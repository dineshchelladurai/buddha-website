03 — THE STUDIO

Calm at the centre, gold at the edges.

We began in 2019 with one rented mandap, a borrowed pickup, and a florist who is now our head of design. The name came later — from the stillness we kept noticing at the heart of every celebration we loved: the moment before the varmala, the first cry of a newborn, the lights coming up on a sixtieth birthday.

Today we are a full decor atelier — florists, carpenters, light designers, drapers and stylists, all in-house, all answerable to one standard: if it isn't good enough for our own family's function, it doesn't leave the workshop.

FLORALSFresh marigolds, jasmine and rose — sourced every morning, never cold-stored overnight.
BUILDOur own carpentry unit — mandaps, swings, canopies and stages built to your plan.
INSTALLOvernight transformations. Lock the venue at 10 PM; wake up to a different world.
TEAMOne crew from first sketch to last aarti. Nothing is subcontracted away.
